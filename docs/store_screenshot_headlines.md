## Ranking and conversion notes

- If you can regenerate screenshots, move a scan/upload screen into the first four slots. Scanning is one of the strongest differentiators in the current store copy.
- Prefer one headline and one short supporting line per screenshot instead of dense annotation.
- Keep the first screenshot outcome-focused, not navigation-focused.
- Reuse the same headline structure across iPhone, iPad, phone, and tablet sets for consistency in App Store Connect and Play Console.
